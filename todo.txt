# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Act as a senior enterprise software architect, Laravel developer, manufacturing ERP consultant, database architect, financial systems specialist, and UI/UX designer.

Your task is to design and develop a complete, production-ready Manufacturing ERP application using Laravel and MySQL.

The application must be capable of managing the complete operations of a manufacturing company, starting from raw material procurement and ending with finished goods sales, delivery, accounting, and profitability analysis.

This must be a fully functional ERP, not a dashboard prototype, static UI, or collection of disconnected CRUD pages.

The system should provide capabilities comparable to established manufacturing platforms such as:
- ERPNext Manufacturing.
- Odoo Manufacturing.
- SAP Business One manufacturing workflows.
- Zoho Inventory and operations management.

However, create an independent, modern, user-friendly application with its own architecture and design.

## Target Market

Primary market: India.

Target industries:
- Food processing.
- Pharmaceutical manufacturing.
- Garment and textile manufacturing.
- Furniture manufacturing.
- Electronics manufacturing.
- Automobile component manufacturing.
- Plastic manufacturing.
- Metal fabrication.
- Packaging manufacturing.
- Chemical manufacturing.
- FMCG manufacturing.
- Engineering and machinery manufacturing.
- Electrical equipment manufacturing.
- Construction material manufacturing.
- Printing and paper manufacturing.

The architecture must support different manufacturing industries through configurable workflows, product attributes, units of measurement, production processes, quality parameters, and inventory rules.

# 23. IMPORTANT BUSINESS LOGIC AND DATA INTEGRITY

These rules are mandatory.

## 23.1 Inventory Integrity

1. Every stock movement must have a source document.
2. Stock transactions must be recorded atomically.
3. Stock must not be issued beyond available quantity unless explicitly configured and authorized.
4. Batch-tracked products must preserve batch identity.
5. Serial-tracked products must preserve individual serial identity.
6. Reversed transactions must create corresponding reversal entries.
7. Historical stock movements must not be silently deleted.
8. Concurrent stock transactions must not cause overselling or double allocation.

## 23.2 Manufacturing Integrity

1. Every production order must reference an approved BOM version.
2. Every production order must reference the relevant routing version.
3. Material issues must be linked to a production order.
4. Material returns must reduce actual consumption.
5. Production output must not exceed authorized limits without an approved exception.
6. Scrap and rejected goods must be separately recorded.
7. Quality-held products must not become available for unrestricted sale.
8. Production completion must account for WIP and finished goods.
9. Historical production costs must remain traceable.

## 23.3 Financial Integrity

1. Every posted journal entry must balance.
2. Every posted invoice must have a unique invoice number within its configured numbering scope.
3. Payments must be allocated to valid invoices or recorded as advances.
4. Tax must be calculated using configured rules.
5. Financial transactions must be auditable.
6. Posted transactions must be reversed through controlled entries.
7. Closed accounting periods must prevent unauthorized backdated postings.

## 23.4 Transaction Safety

Use Laravel database transactions for critical operations.

Use appropriate locking or atomic updates for:
- Stock reservations.
- Stock issues.
- GRN posting.
- Production receipts.
- Invoice posting.
- Payment allocation.
- Journal posting.

Avoid race conditions and duplicate posting.

Implement idempotency controls for operations that could be retried by background jobs or API requests.
